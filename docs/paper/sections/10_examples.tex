\subsection{A thermal model with Therminator3}
\label{sec:example-therminator}

This example compares events generated by Therminator3 (Section~\ref{sec:therminator}) with events generated by
THERMINATOR~2.0.3~\cite{Chojnacki:2011hb} with the same freeze-out models and parameters. Two configurations are used:
the blast wave of THERMINATOR~2 (\code{fomodel/blastwave.ini}) and the (2+1)-dimensional hydrodynamic hypersurface of
Au--Au collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}} = 200$~GeV, 20--30\% centrality, of its library
(\code{RHICAuAu200c2030Ti455ti025Tf145.xml}). In both programs the integration uses $2\times10^6$ points per hadron.
The events of THERMINATOR~2 are read with the same analysis tasks as those of Therminator3. Figure~\ref{fig:t3-vs-t2}
compares, for $\pi^+$, $K^+$, $p$ and $\Lambda$, the mean multiplicities, the $\pT$ spectra and the rapidity
distributions, for the primordial hadrons and after the decays of the resonances. Table~\ref{tab:t3-vs-t2} lists the
mean multiplicities and mean $\pT$.
